**The convention's contract in five lines**
1. Persistent knowledge goes to the substrate via `ffs_author_atom` or `~/.ffs/ingest/`; search first with `ffs_search` and extend existing entities rather than duplicating.
2. Progressive disclosure: listings and search hits, then one rendered projection, then atoms only when provenance or `as_of` is needed; never dump the substrate or walk `~/.ffs/`.
3. Provenance is honest: set `source_uri` to the real origin, mark inferences as inferences, never strip engine/model provenance, never present content as owner-authored.
4. The owner is the gate: supersession preserves history, conflicts are stated not resolved, rejected proposals are not re-proposed without new evidence, and a capability denial is the answer.
5. A `submission_id` means proposed, not saved; the agent reports "proposed, awaiting daily-summary review" and never claims persistence that did not happen.
