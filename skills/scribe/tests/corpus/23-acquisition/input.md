Kestrel Building Systems said Friday it has acquired Oakline Millwork, a Monroe cabinet maker, for an undisclosed sum.

Oakline founder Ruth Calloway will step down as chief executive at closing. Kestrel chief executive Ben Tran said the Oakline plant will keep operating.
