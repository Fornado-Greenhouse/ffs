Brightloom Analytics, a Charlotte software company, has raised $24 million in Series B funding led by Copperfield Ventures with participation from Meridian Growth Partners.

Chief executive Priya Raman said the money will fund hiring. Copperfield partner Tomas Eyre joins the board.
