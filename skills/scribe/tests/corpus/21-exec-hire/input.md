Pinewood Freight, a Gastonia trucking company, said Thursday it has named Dana Whitfield chief operating officer, effective Oct. 1. Whitfield joins from Ridgeline Carriers, where she led regional operations.

She succeeds Lee Marsh, who stepped down Sept. 30 after six years.
